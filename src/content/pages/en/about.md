## Hi there, I'm Damian Cebulak!

On a daily basis, I work as a **Tech Lead and Senior Analyst**, specializing in the design and transformation of complex core banking systems and IT architectures in banking. Over a decade in the industry, I have had the opportunity to tackle challenges related to high availability, stringent transactional requirements, technical debt, and the need to maintain consistency in large organizations.

Beyond the commercial IT world, I am actively involved in developing the analytical community as the **Head of Marketing at IIBA Chapter Poland**. I believe that sharing knowledge, popularizing good practices, and building spaces for discussion are the best ways to elevate the quality of the entire industry.

## About This Blog (Knowledge Garden)

This space is my **digital garden**—a living experiment used for organizing knowledge, testing modern tech stacks, and exploring complex socio-technical systems.

### What is the Digital Garden Concept?
Unlike traditional blogs, where posts are chronological, closed articles (resembling a news feed or journal), a **digital garden** adopts a non-linear knowledge base format. Notes do not age as quickly there. They are constantly tended, developed, supplemented, and linked together into a network of related concepts. It is a space where content evolves alongside my understanding of a topic: from loose thoughts to structured guides.

I treat this blog as an experimental testbed for refining concepts, documenting architectural patterns, and reflecting on engineering challenges. Some of the content is assisted by artificial intelligence tools, but I always strive to bring as much of myself as possible, basing the materials on my real experiences, insights, and project practice.

Although the project was created primarily as a personal knowledge base, I hope these notes, guides, and analyses will also help others in the fields of **business analysis, systems analysis, and digital transformation**.
